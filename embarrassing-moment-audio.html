Last week, my mum and I were shopping in the town.

We wanted to buy a present for my brother's birthday.

Suddenly, I turned around, but I couldn't find my mother.

I shouted to her loudly. All the people looked at me.

I felt embarrassed. Suddenly, my mother came to me.

I had lost her. I felt so embarrassed and upset.

I wished I hadn't turned around. I hugged her tightly, and we went back to the shop together.
