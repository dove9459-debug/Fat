A. Listen, read and find out

1. Who is telling the story?

Johnny.

2. Where are Jad and Johnny?

They are in London and taking a DUCK tour around the city.

3. Why do people laugh at Johnny?

He thinks the bus is going to drive into the water. He doesn't know it's a bus and a boat.

2. Answer the questions

a. In which city are Jad and Johnny?

They are in London.

b. Why are they there?

They have won a competition and are part of an editorial team.

c. What do the new friends decide to do?

They decide to take a DUCK tour.

d. Why is the DUCK tour special?

The bus turns into a boat.

e. What were the two embarrassing things that happened to Johnny during the tour?

He was frightened because he thought the bus was going to drive into the river. He opened the wrong door and got wet.

f. Why wasn't Johnny embarrassed?

It gave him something to write about.
