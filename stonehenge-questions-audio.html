1. Why was Stonehenge built?

No one knows. Probably to study the sky, but some people say it was a place for aliens to land.

2. Some people think Stonehenge wasn't built by humans. Who do they think built it?

Aliens, giants.

3. What makes Stonehenge surprising?

The stones are very big and heavy. No one knows how people 4,000 years ago moved the stones.

4. How does Stonehenge make Johnny feel?

Scared.

5. Why is the group camping at Stonehenge?

To see the sunrise.

6. Why were Johnny and Aziz afraid of a sheep?

They thought it might be aliens or a giant.
